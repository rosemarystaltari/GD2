what's the point?

of creating?

same as before.

to express.

to release.

in doing so,

the artist's drive of

emotion bleeds

through

the final piece

to the viewer,

in turn transferring

these strong feelings

to those who it may

resonate.

the point then shifts from

artist to viewer,

a means of

communication,

a means of

inspiration of thought,

emotion,

and drive.
